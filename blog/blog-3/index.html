Taking the GRE and IELTS to Study Data Science Abroad

By David Gadyan · Published 10 March 2022 · Updated 29 August 2026 · 1 min read · gre, ielts, education, data science, masters

Key takeaways

  • GRE and IELTS are standard entry requirements for most international data science master’s programmes.
  • The GRE quantitative section is a direct test of the maths foundations data science relies on, so preparing for it is not wasted effort.
  • Continuous education compounds. A degree taken while already working brings problems back to the classroom and solutions back to the work.

Frequently asked questions

What exams do you need for a data science master's abroad?
Most international programmes require the GRE (general aptitude, with a quantitative section) and a proof of English proficiency such as IELTS or TOEFL. Specific score requirements vary by school and programme.
Is GRE preparation useful beyond admissions?
The quantitative section covers algebra, geometry, probability and data interpretation — the same foundations that machine learning is built on. Preparing for it doubles as a structured revision of the maths a data scientist uses.
