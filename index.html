FREQUENTLY ASKED

What does David Gadyan do?

David Gadyan is a data scientist, agentic AI developer and AI marketing specialist with 8 years of experience and 180+ delivered projects. He builds AI and machine learning systems that reach production - agentic workflows, RAG pipelines, computer vision, forecasting and full-stack data applications - and sets up the analytics and conversion tracking that prove those systems moved a business number.

What services do you offer?

Nine core services: generative AI and agentic systems, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, web development, web scraping, database management, software development, and MLOps/DevOps. On the marketing side: GA4 and conversion tracking setup, tracking audits, marketing analytics and AI automation for lead handling.

How much does a project cost?

It depends entirely on scope, so there is no list price. Small, well-defined pieces of work - a tracking audit, a scraper, a proof-of-concept agent - are typically a few days. Production systems are scoped as projects with milestones. The fastest way to a real number is a 15-minute call: describe the problem and you will get an honest range, including whether it is worth doing at all.

How long does a project take?

A proof of concept is usually one to two weeks. A production deployment - model, API, monitoring and handover - is typically four to eight weeks depending on data readiness. Audits and tracking fixes are measured in days, not weeks. Data access is almost always the thing that decides the timeline, not the modelling.

What technologies do you work with?

Python, R, JavaScript and SQL as core languages. For AI: LangChain, LangGraph, RAG architectures with vector stores such as Pinecone, and the major LLM APIs. For interfaces: React, Next.js, React Native and Python Dash. For serving: Flask and FastAPI REST APIs. For operations: DevOps/MLOps on cloud infrastructure including AWS, with models deployed and monitored in live environments.

Can you build an AI agent or chatbot for my business?

Yes - this is the core of the current work. That includes retrieval-augmented generation over your own documents, multi-step agents that take actions rather than just answer, workflow automation with tools such as n8n, and customer-facing assistants. The chat window you are reading right now is a small example.

Why is my Google Analytics not recording any conversions?

Usually one of three causes. The measurement ID on the site does not match the property you are reading. Or the event fires but was never marked as a key event in GA4, so it is stored and never counted. Or the conversion happens off your domain - a form that posts to a third party and lands the visitor on someone else's thank-you page - so no pageview of yours ever records it. All three are quick to diagnose and quick to fix.

Do you offer a tracking or analytics audit?

Yes. The audit checks whether every enquiry path on your site produces a countable event, whether your key events are actually marked as conversions, what your page weight is costing you in paid traffic, and what search engines and AI assistants currently say you do. You get findings ranked by payoff per hour, each with a free way to verify it yourself.
